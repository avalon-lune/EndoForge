## Phase 4 and collision correction

The correction addressed direct curved retreat through contact-feature generation and mapped-state synchronization after rebuild/rollback. The 19-case path/angle/speed matrix adds whole-face and intermediate-state checks while retaining cutting compatibility. Flat and curved endurance each cover 600 simulated seconds and 60 grip cycles. See [PHASE4](PHASE4.md), [collision correction](PHASE4-COLLISION-FIX.md) and [public summaries](verification/phase4-summary.json).

## Phase 5

I introduced original editable anatomy and authoring scripts; the tutor's reference packages and patient images are not distributed here. Gallbladder volume FEM, neck constraints and compliant liver-bed connections support small coordinated two-grasper traction, release and replay. Short paired image/geometry exports establish basic output readiness. Modeling existed at this stage; final visual realism did not. See [PHASE5](PHASE5.md) and [results](verification/phase5-summary.json).

## Phase 6

The common foundation improves manual target/stop bounds, material-bound approach presets, observation space, detailed bound display meshes, textures/materials, actual advanced rendering and exact-state exports before the research projects diverge.

Physical work includes sustained bilateral grip, removing numerical contact softening that caused creep, adaptive accepted substeps/contact-cache reconstruction, static-liver jaw admission and guarded replay. The native suite contains 35 cases, including 15 long holds and 50 continuous cycles. The r2 editor/package/fresh-extraction runs retain both grasps during 2 mm per-tool traction, release, record/replay and export 24 stereo frames.

[Status and r2 correction](STATUS.md), [technical scope](PHASE6.md), [acceptance requirements](../Scripts/Phase6/acceptance-spec.json) and [recorded results](verification/phase6-summary.json) distinguish intended gates from completed tests.

## Phase 7

The Phase 6 baseline remains fixed. The new physical liver and corresponding display were requalified with the complete 35-case native suite, sustained-grasp and independent geometry checks. Original appearance changes soften angular contours and vessel marks, revise regional tissue colour and fat detail, and preserve explicit geometry/normal/ID correspondence. Strict replay compares stable requested/effective execution settings while retaining transient SOFA fields as diagnostics.

The local package and a fresh ZIP extraction both passed actual interaction, replay, rendering and 24-frame stereo checks. The fresh extraction measured 59.65 FPS and 0.9757 simulated seconds per live wall second. See [the Phase 7 scope](PHASE7.md) and [exact results](verification/phase7-summary.json). These engineering gates do not imply surgical photorealism, calibrated tissue forces or clinician-approved anatomy.

## Phase 8

Engineering-only; appearance remains synthetic and clinical/anatomy review remains pending. The exact accepted Phase7 35-case native qualification is inherited, not rerun as Phase8. Four frozen stereo frames are internal alignment regressions. No specialized Project1/Project2 branch development, research sequences or teammate-reader integration is delivered. See [scope](PHASE8.md) and [branch responsibilities](PHASE8-BRANCH-BOUNDARY.md).

## Phase 9

The changed gallbladder wall has 724 physical nodes and 2,160 tetrahedra; its
92,160-triangle display follows accepted physical states within a recorded
0.12 mm reconstruction bound. Revised contour, shading normals, regional
appearance and surrounding tissue reduce the conspicuous angular lower edge.
Grey near views retain shallow curvature bands for human review.

New state fields expose actual node velocities and signed world-coordinate
per-jaw contact reactions, with explicit units and last-accepted-substep
validity. The older sum of contact-force magnitudes remains distinct.

This physical change received fresh qualification: 35 native cases, all 62,958
states of 15 sustained holds, 26 reference/matrix/nominal geometry audits and
full endurance contact/liver checks. Actual packaged and fresh-extracted runs
passed eight independent capture audits each. Fresh extraction measured
56.09 FPS and a 0.954748 simulation/wall ratio. Four paired frames are internal
correspondence checks. See [scope](PHASE9.md), [exact results](verification/phase9-summary.json)
and [the physical-output contract](../Physics/README-Phase9-Output.md).

Engineering verification and local packaging are complete. Human visual and
interaction approval remain pending; clinical calibration, suturing, full organ
dissection and the research projects' specialized branches are not delivered.
