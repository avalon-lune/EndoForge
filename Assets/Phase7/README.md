# Phase 7 common anatomy appearance

Author and maintainer: **Avalon / EndoForge**.

This is the shared scene foundation before the two research projects specialize.
`EndoForge_Anatomy.blend` contains editable original meshes and materials, with
relative image links under `textures/`. `anatomy-preview.png` is a Blender
authoring preview; actual UE/package captures are separate acceptance evidence.

## What changes

- Original irregular posterior serosal pleats and lower cavity folds replace
  repetitive sinusoidal context. Original continuous omental sheets have
  variable ellipsoidal lobules, coherent larger folds and yellow/cream material
  variation. These remain non-contact context, with the same object IDs.
- The liver is rebuilt as a continuous closed physical surface, removing the
  inherited equatorial discontinuity and sharp bed shoulder. Its collider has
  7,432 vertices and 14,860 triangles; the largest adjacent-face angle falls from
  109.54 to 27.78 degrees and the 95th percentile from 31.66 to 11.34 degrees.
  A smooth interpolating bed retains all 31 anchor positions exactly. A continuous
  operative UV chart removes the texture pole at the hepatic bed.
- The gallbladder retains the reference's pale ivory/yellow-grey appearance,
  with pink/grey regional variation and tapered, softly buried vascular marks.
  Liver capsule, fat, connective bed and cavity receive distinct original
  multiscale colour, roughness and normal textures. Bright highlights are
  generated by lighting/BRDF; none are painted into colour maps.
- All static render assets use `/Game/Phase7/Meshes`; material assets use
  `/Game/Phase7/Materials`. No Phase 5 or Phase 6 source asset is overwritten.

## Mechanical and data contract

The physical identity is new and requires full native requalification. The
gallbladder FEM, constitutive parameters, thickness, constraints and 31 liver-bed
anchor positions retain Phase 6 values exactly. The liver collider and its
triangle bindings are rebuilt. The original ready poses and rig are retained exactly. A smooth posterior
fossa fitted to accepted operative heights preserves the working space for
rolled open approach and closure, with the unchanged 0.15 mm guard. These remain original engineering anatomy, **not patient anatomy**.

The gallbladder PN display remains unchanged. The liver display is rebuilt from
the new collider with 59,440 triangles, maximum 0.12 mm offset and a locked 5 mm
anchor neighborhood. Normal textures and the liver normal filter modify shading
only. Exported geometric normals describe actual
triangles and must not be confused with shader normals. Every visible context
mesh has matching exported geometry and a stable ID. A static rest-organ
clearance screen is not a proof for every user-driven tool path; moving-scene
contact, image and depth audits remain required.

`scene.json` retains the physical model/version and introduces a separate
`appearance_identity` and `phase7_render` profile. Model, texture, camera,
lighting and actual rendered-geometry identities must be captured with data.
No camera distortion, sensor noise, compression or project-specific dataset
contract is introduced by these assets.

## Authoring and checks

From the repository root, use the installed Blender executable:

```powershell
& 'E:/Software/Blender/blender.exe' -b --python Scripts/BlenderPhase7/build_assets.py -- --render
& 'E:/Software/Blender/blender.exe' -b --python Scripts/BlenderPhase7/verify_blend.py
python Scripts/BlenderPhase7/audit_liver_geometry.py
& 'E:/Software/Blender/blender.exe' -b --python Scripts/BlenderPhase7/audit_assets.py
python Scripts/BlenderPhase7/audit_contract.py
```

These commands write only Phase 7 assets/reports. Blender reopen verifies
editable geometry and relative textures. The independent structural audit
reuses preserved FBX/binding kernels and checks retained Phase 6 gallbladder
values, the original rig, exact anchor reconstruction, new closed
liver self-intersections and whole-triangle gallbladder clearance, separate asset
namespace, shading normals and context clearance. NumPy is required for the
liver audit. These rest-state checks do not qualify dynamic behavior. UE import
and actual packaged/fresh-extracted runtime acceptance are additional gates.

Teacher-supplied laparoscopic frames and the two anatomy atlases were reviewed
for form, tissue relationships and material appearance. No photographic pixels
or source meshes are embedded here. The source references are not redistributed.
Their compressed images do not establish calibrated optical properties.
Anatomical, optical and clinical review remains pending.
